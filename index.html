BIO

A comedian who thinks claps are cheap, Alexander goes for laughs. All bits are meticulously written, worked, reworked, and revised. Performances are flexible and conversational, keeping audiences fully engrossed between punchlines.

Confident in commanding a room with playful quick wit, Alexander is unafraid to calmly and comedically de-escalate any heckling incidents. Distractions become tools, not obstacles, as Alexander keeps the entire audience locked in.

Whether delivering one-liners, wordplay, deadpan, or fabricated anecdotes, Alexander brings sharp, thoughtful comedy without pandering.
